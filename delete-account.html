Delete Your Account

You can request the deletion of your NIRVE account and all associated data at any time. This page explains what data will be deleted and how to submit your request.

STEP 1
Send a deletion request
Send an email to our privacy team using the address below. Include the email address associated with your NIRVE account in your message.
STEP 2
Verification
We will verify your identity by confirming the email address associated with your account. This step protects your account from unauthorised deletion requests.
STEP 3
Deletion processed
Once verified, your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.
DATA THAT WILL BE DELETED
  • Your account credentials and profile information
  • All writing session history and scores
  • Your Writing Performance Profile and Band Prediction data
  • All weakness memory records stored in our database
  • Any data associated with your account in our systems
DATA STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE
  • Theme preference (localStorage)
  • Prompt rotation history (localStorage)
  • PWA install preference (localStorage)

This data is stored on your device only and is not held by NIRVE. You can clear it by clearing your browser or app data.

Send your deletion request to
info@niracademy.com
⏱ Processed within 30 days of verification
